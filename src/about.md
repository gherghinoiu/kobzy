At **Kobzy** we do used cars the way they should be done: honestly.

Kobzy is run by a **qualified mechanic**. Every car is personally hand-picked,
then inspected and put right in the workshop before it's listed — so by the time
you see it, it starts, drives and stops with no faults. We describe our cars
exactly as they are, price them fairly against the market, and never use pushy
sales tactics. If a car has a mark or something worth knowing, we'll tell you —
because a happy customer is a returning customer.

## Why buy from us

- **Prepared by a mechanic** — inspected and sorted in the workshop before it's listed.
- **Full working order** — it starts, drives and stops with no faults.
- **Full transparency** — plenty of real photos, and video with engine sound on request.
- **No pressure** — take your time, ask questions, request a video call.
- **UK-wide delivery** — can't reach us in Northampton? We'll arrange delivery.

## How it works

1. Browse our [current stock](/#stock).
2. Message us on WhatsApp for more photos, a video, or to book a viewing.
3. Reserve, pay, and drive away — or we deliver it to you.
