At **Kobzy** we do used cars the way they should be done: honestly.

Every car we list is hand-picked, mechanically checked and HPI-verified before
it reaches our stock. We describe our cars accurately, price them fairly against
the market, and never use pushy sales tactics. If a car has a mark or a service
coming up, we'll tell you — because a happy customer is a returning customer.

## Why buy from us

- **Fully-checked stock** — inspected and HPI-clear before listing.
- **Full transparency** — real photos and video walkarounds on every car.
- **Fair pricing** — sensibly priced, with finance and part-exchange available.
- **No pressure** — take your time, ask questions, request a video call.
- **UK-wide delivery** — can't reach us? We'll arrange delivery.

## How it works

1. Browse our [current stock](/#stock).
2. Message us on WhatsApp for more photos, a video, or to book a viewing.
3. Reserve, arrange finance or part-exchange, and drive away — or we deliver.

Got a question? [Get in touch](/contact/) — we're happy to help.
